FAQ

Questions, answered

What is Specto?

Specto is a free Chrome extension for inspecting websites. Hover any element to see its CSS, box model, spacing, fonts and colors, measure distances in pixels, check WCAG contrast, edit the live page and test responsive layouts, all without opening DevTools.

Is Specto really free?

Yes. Specto is completely free, with no account, no trial and no limits.

How is Specto different from Chrome DevTools?

DevTools is built for debugging code. Specto is built for checking design: one hover shows the values a designer or front-end developer needs, in a Figma-style card, with redlines, rulers, layout grids and comments on top of the live page.

Who is Specto for?

UI/UX designers, front-end developers, design agencies, QA and design reviewers, freelancers, and Webflow, Framer and WordPress builders who need to compare a live website with its design.

Does Specto collect my data?

No. Specto only runs on a tab when you click it. Comments, annotations, guides and settings are saved in your own browser, and nothing is sent anywhere.

Can Specto export a design system for AI coding agents?

Yes. Specto reads the live page and writes a DESIGN.md with its colors, typography, spacing, radius, shadows and components, plus a SKILL.md you can give to Claude Code, Cursor or Antigravity.

Can I download images and SVG icons from a website?

Yes. Press D to see every image, inline SVG, CSS background, favicon and video poster on the page with its format, size and dimensions. Download one, copy the SVG code, or save them all as a .zip. A few servers block direct downloads; those open in a new tab so you can save them.

Can I compare my website with other websites?

Yes. In Responsive mode, switch to Compare and pick Mobile, Tablet or Desktop. Add sites by URL and they load side by side at that size. Other sites are view only, and some sites block being shown inside another page.

Which browsers does Specto work in?

Specto works in Google Chrome and other Chromium-based browsers.
